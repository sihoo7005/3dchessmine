How to Play

Cells Open
Double-tap a closed cell.
Flag
Long-press or right-click a cell. In Flag mode, click or tap once.
Piece range
Select an open piece to show its range.
Open range
Double-tap an open piece. The flag count must match its number. Incorrect flags may open a mine.
Auto-open
Double-tap an empty area to open the ranges of pieces whose numbers match their flag counts.
Fullscreen
Use the fullscreen button to enlarge or restore the game view.
View controls
Drag to rotate; use the wheel or two fingers to zoom.
2D view
Scroll to view each level in 2D mode.
Reasoning hint
Press the button to show a cell you can infer and its related clues.
Auto Group
Premium users can long-press an open piece or select it and press Auto Group to group its remaining cells and mines. Flags count as mines.
Color Groups
In Create Group mode, view an open piece’s range, select closed cells, enter the mine count, and save. All groups are shown, and their counts change as cells are flagged or opened.
Keyboard
V: view · F: flag · R: new game · Arrow keys: rotate · + / −: zoom
