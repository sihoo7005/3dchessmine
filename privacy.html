Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

Operator sihoo7005 handles personal information for 3D Chess Minesweeper as described below. Contact: sihoo7005@gmail.com

1. Purposes and data collected

Google sign-in is optional. If you sign in, we use the account identifier, email address, and profile name provided by Google to identify your account and authenticate you. We store the mode, board size, mine count, piece weights, result, duration, and completion time of games completed while signed in so you can view your history. We also store each account’s remaining hint balance.

The board, game state, flags, and groups are handled in your browser; game progress is not stored on the server. Games played while signed out are not saved to the server. Guest hints reset when you refresh or close the page. Records that fail to upload may be lost when you close the page.

2. Retention and deletion

We retain accounts, game history, and hint balances until the account is deleted. Select “Delete account and history” in the game’s Account menu to remove the account and its history and hint data from the server database. We do not currently maintain separate backups. We retain information for any additional period required by law.

3. Cookies and browser storage

During sign-in, the server uses cookies for sessions and security. After sign-in, authentication data is kept in the browser tab’s session storage and remains valid for up to 12 hours. Signing out deletes that data. The game’s double-tap setting is stored in browser local storage. You can delete or block cookies and storage data in your browser settings, but sign-in or saved settings may not work properly.

4. Service providers

Game pages are hosted on Cloudflare Pages and GitHub Pages. Sign-in and game history are handled by an Oracle Cloud server. Google provides sign-in authentication. These providers may process relevant information to provide and secure their services. Please review each provider’s policies for details.

Cloudflare Privacy Policy · GitHub Pages data collection notice · GitHub Privacy Policy · Google Privacy Policy · Oracle Privacy Policy

5. Promotions and advertising

The screen shown to earn hints is an in-house promotion for another game by the operator. We do not currently use Google advertising code. We will update this policy if advertising services or data practices change.

6. Contact and your rights

You may request access to, correction or deletion of your personal information, or restriction of its processing, as provided by applicable law. For privacy inquiries, contact operator sihoo7005 at sihoo7005@gmail.com. For information handled directly by GitHub or Google, please consult their respective guidance.

7. Changes to this policy

If our data practices change, we will update this page and its effective date.
